Frequently Asked Questions

What are the best rated bath towels for most people?

For most homes, the KAHAF Collection 6-Pack at $23.99 is the best rated bath towels pick. You get six soft, absorbent 100% cotton towels for about $4 each, which covers a couple or a small family with a full rotation.

Are 100% cotton towels better than cotton-polyester blends?

Yes, for absorbency and feel. Pure cotton holds more water and softens with washing, while polyester blends resist water and can feel slick after a few cycles. We chose cotton for every adult towel in this guide.

How often should you wash bath towels?

Wash bath towels after three or four uses, and hang them to dry fully between uses. Damp towels grow bacteria and start to smell, so airflow matters as much as the wash itself.

Why are my new towels not absorbent?

New towels carry a factory sizing finish that repels water. Wash them once before first use with detergent and no fabric softener, and they will absorb the way they should.

What size bath towel should I buy?

A standard bath towel runs about 27 by 54 inches and wraps an adult. Smaller 22 by 44 inch towels dry faster and store easier, while bath sheets are larger but slower to dry. For kids, look for the toddler and baby sizes in our picks above.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Turkish cotton actually better than Egyptian cotton for bath towels?

It depends on what you're optimizing for. Turkish cotton has long fibers that give towels a dense, plush feel, while Egyptian cotton is prized for similar fiber length but a tighter, more structured weave. Neither is objectively better, and the right choice in this Brooklinen 100% Turkish Cotton review comes down to whether you value density or a more structured hand.

How much more does the Brooklinen towel cost than the alternatives?

Brooklinen's two-pack runs $109.00, or $54.50 per towel. Impressions' Egyptian cotton towels cost $90.90 each, and the Fabdreams organic cotton towel costs $109.99, so Brooklinen sits in the middle of this comparison rather than at either price extreme.

Does a Turkish cotton towel need special care compared to regular cotton towels?

Turkish cotton doesn't require different washing equipment, but owners report it keeps its density best when washed in warm rather than hot water and dried on a lower heat setting. That care pattern applies to most 100% cotton towels, including the Egyptian cotton and organic cotton alternatives in this comparison.

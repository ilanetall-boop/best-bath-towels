Things to Know Before You Buy

  • Material drives everything. Microfiber dries in minutes and packs small, so it suits sweat-wiping during a session. Cotton feels plusher and works better for a post-shower dry-off, but it stays damp longer in a closed bag.
  • Size matters more than you think. A 48 by 24 inch towel like the Rainleaf covers a bench and still clips to a bag. Full bath sets give you more coverage but eat locker space.
  • Buy in multiples. You sweat through a gym towel every session, so a 4 to 8 piece set keeps a clean one ready while the others are in the wash.
  • Skip fabric softener. It coats the fibers and cuts absorbency, which is the one job a gym towel has to do well.
  • Price per towel is the real number. A $35.99 four-pack often beats a single $12.99 towel once you factor in how many you need in rotation.

Frequently Asked Questions

What size hand towel is best for the gym?

For wiping sweat between sets, a compact towel works best because it tucks into a gym bag and dries fast. The Rainleaf Microfiber measures 48 by 24 inches, large enough to drape over a bench yet light enough to clip to your bag. If you want more coverage after a shower, a standard cotton towel around 30 by 54 inches gives you room without weighing down your locker.

Is microfiber or cotton better for a gym towel?

Microfiber wins for gym use because it absorbs quickly, packs down small, and dries before your next session, which keeps mildew odor out of your bag. Cotton feels softer against the skin and works well if you shower at the gym and want a plush dry-off, but it stays damp longer. We picked the Rainleaf Microfiber for sweat duty and added cotton sets like the Tens Towels for people who want a softer post-shower towel.

How often should you wash a gym towel?

Wash your gym towel after every workout. Sweat feeds bacteria, and a towel that sits damp in a bag turns sour within a day. Microfiber options like the Rainleaf handle frequent machine washing without losing absorbency, so a daily cycle does not shorten their life the way it can with thicker cotton. Skip fabric softener, which coats fibers and cuts how much sweat the towel can pull off your skin.

Do I need more than one gym towel?

Yes. Because you should wash a gym towel after each session, a single towel leaves you stuck on laundry day. The best hand towels for gym routines come in sets for this reason, like the Tens Towels 4-pack or the Utopia 8-piece, so a clean towel is always ready while the others are in the wash.

How do I stop my gym towel from smelling?

Never let it sit damp in a closed bag, which is where the smell starts. Dry it out as soon as you get home, wash it after every use, and skip fabric softener so the fibers keep their full absorbency. Microfiber dries fastest, so it gives bacteria the least time to grow, which is part of why the Rainleaf is our top pick.

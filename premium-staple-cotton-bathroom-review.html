What is the difference between staple cotton and Egyptian cotton towels?

Is 100% cotton better than microfiber for bath towels?

It depends on what you want from a towel. Cotton sets like the One Thousand Reasons pick and Madison Park's organic option absorb more water per towel and feel more traditional against skin. POLYTE's 430 GSM microfiber alternative dries faster and packs smaller, and owners report that matters more for travel or gym use than for everyday bathroom drying, where most buyers still default to cotton.

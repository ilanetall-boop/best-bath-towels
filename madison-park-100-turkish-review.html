Frequently Asked Questions

Is Madison Park's Turkish cotton better than Egyptian cotton for bath towels?

It depends on what you want from a towel. Turkish cotton, the fiber behind the Madison Park set, has a looser weave that dries faster and is known for softening with repeated washing. Egyptian cotton, used in the Superior set in this comparison, is denser and prized for long fibers that hold more water per towel. Neither fiber is objectively better, and the right pick comes down to whether you value quicker drying or maximum absorbency.

Does Turkish cotton need special washing care compared to other cotton towels?

Turkish cotton doesn't require different washing equipment than a standard cotton towel, but owners researching the fiber consistently report it needs a few wash cycles before it reaches its signature soft hand. Washing in warm water and skipping high dryer heat helps the fibers soften evenly instead of matting, the same care pattern that applies to most 100% cotton towel sets, including Superior's Egyptian cotton option.

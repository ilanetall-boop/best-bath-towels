Frequently Asked Questions

Is 100% organic cotton actually better than regular cotton for bath towels?

It depends on what you're optimizing for. Organic cotton is grown without synthetic pesticides and processed without the chlorine bleach and formaldehyde-based softeners common in conventional cotton finishing, which matters if you have sensitive skin or want fewer chemical residues on fabric that touches your body daily. Absorbency and durability come down more to the weave and gram weight than to organic certification alone.

How much more does an organic cotton bath towel set cost?

The Fabdreams 100% Organic Cotton Bath Towel set runs $109.99 for six pieces, or about $18.33 per towel. Will an organic cotton towel shrink or lose softness after washing?

Untreated organic cotton can shrink more in its first few hot washes than conventionally finished cotton, since it skips some of the resin treatments that stabilize fiber dimensions. Owners consistently recommend washing in warm or cool water and skipping high heat in the dryer to keep the loops from tightening and the towel from stiffening over time.

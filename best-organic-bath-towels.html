Frequently Asked Questions

What makes a bath towel organic?

A bath towel earns the organic label when it is made from cotton grown without synthetic pesticides or fertilizers, usually backed by a certification like GOTS or OEKO-TEX. The word organic on its own, without a named standard, tells you little, so check the listing for specifics rather than taking the title at face value.

How do I wash cotton bath towels to keep them soft?

Wash them in warm water with about half the detergent you think you need, and skip the fabric softener entirely, since it coats the fibers and cuts absorbency over time. Tumble dry on medium heat and pull the towels out slightly damp rather than bone dry to keep the pile from turning stiff.

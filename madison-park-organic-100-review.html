The Ivory colorway of the Madison Park Organic 100% Cotton set is the version we focus on for this Madison Park Organic 100% review, priced at $73.35 for six pieces. Together those two certifications cover both the raw material and the finished product, which is more documentation than most towels labeled organic actually provide.

How does this Madison Park Organic 100% review's pick compare to the Seafoam version?

Is the low-twist construction in this towel set worth the tradeoff?

It depends on how you use your towels. If you wash towels several times a week for a busy household, that durability tradeoff is worth weighing against the certifications this Madison Park set offers.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between a bath sheet and a bath towel?

A bath sheet is simply a larger bath towel. A standard bath towel runs about 27 by 52 inches, while a bath sheet stretches closer to 35 by 70 inches, like the White Classic in this guide. The extra fabric wraps fully around an adult body and dries you faster, which is why people size up. The tradeoff is that bath sheets take longer to dry on the rail and fill more of your laundry load.

Can you get good quality bath sheets for under $30?

Yes. Most of the towels in this guide sell under $30, including the HIPHOP PANDA at $12.99 and the Gucilulu at $9.97, and several of them hold up well through repeated hot washes. You give up a luxury brand name, not absorbency. Look at GSM weight and material, cotton and bamboo for softness, microfiber for fast drying, rather than the label on the tag.

How do I keep budget towels soft after washing?

Skip fabric softener, which coats the fibers and reduces absorbency over time, and use about half the detergent you think you need. Wash on warm, then tumble dry on low and pull the towels out while they still have a little give. A periodic wash with a cup of white vinegar strips built-up residue and revives the loft on cotton picks like the Superior Egyptian cotton.

What is the best bath sheet under $30?

Among the best bath sheets under $30, the POLYTE 430 GSM Microfiber Oversize is our overall pick for its dense weave and quick drying, though it lists slightly above budget at $41.89. If you want to stay strictly under $30, the White Classic Luxury Bath Sheets at $37.99 in a pack, or the under-$15 HIPHOP PANDA and Gucilulu towels, give you the most absorbent fabric for the money.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Egyptian cotton actually worth the extra cost?

Long-staple Egyptian cotton produces stronger, softer yarn than the shorter-staple cotton used in most budget towels, and that difference shows up as a set that keeps its plushness and absorbency through more wash cycles before wearing thin. Whether the premium is worth it to you depends on how often you currently replace towels and how much the hand-feel matters day to day. If you replace towels annually, the extra durability can offset the higher upfront cost.

How much should a heavyweight Egyptian cotton towel set cost?

In this comparison, prices ranged from $49.99 for the LANE LINEN set up to $94.99 for the Westlinen set, with the Impressions pick landing at $93.60 for six pieces. Expect to pay more per towel for genuine heavyweight construction than you would for a standard lightweight cotton set, since the extra material and finishing add real cost rather than just marketing markup.

Do heavyweight towels take longer to dry?

Yes. The dense weave that gives heavyweight Egyptian cotton towels their plush feel also holds more water than a lightweight towel, so they typically need a longer dryer cycle or more time on a towel bar. If your bathroom has limited airflow, factor that extra drying time into your decision before buying a heavyweight set.

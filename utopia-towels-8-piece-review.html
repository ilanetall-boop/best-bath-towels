How does the Utopia Towels 8 Piece set compare to the American Soft Linen or Tens Towels alternatives?

How do you keep ring-spun cotton towels soft over time?

Wash ring-spun cotton towels like these in warm or cold water, skip fabric softener since it coats the fibers and reduces absorbency, and tumble dry on low or medium heat. Owners of similar Utopia cotton sets report that softness holds up better when the towels aren't overloaded in the washer, which lets the fibers rinse and dry fully.

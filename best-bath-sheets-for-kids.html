Frequently Asked Questions

What size bath sheet is best for kids?

It depends on age. A medium hooded towel like the ZICOTO fits kids 3 to 6, a 50-by-32-inch wrap like the FUBANRAY suits toddlers with room to grow, and a 40-inch square such as the KeaBabies is sized for babies. Match the towel to your child's current size rather than buying the biggest option, since the best bath sheets for kids are the ones that actually wrap the body.

Are hooded towels or flat bath sheets better for kids?

Hooded towels keep wet hair off the neck and hold heat, which helps with younger kids who get cold fast. Flat bath sheets give more fabric to wrap an older child. Most of our picks for kids under age 6 are hooded for that warmth.

Is 100% cotton or a cotton blend better for a kids' towel?

100% cotton absorbs faster and resists mildew smell better, so it dries quicker on a hook. A cotton blend like the FUBANRAY can feel plusher but holds a little more moisture. For everyday bath use, we lean toward 100% cotton.

Is organic cotton worth it for kids' towels?

If your child has sensitive skin, yes. Organic cotton like the KeaBabies or Burt's Bees costs more and can feel stiff before its first wash, but it softens quickly and avoids the chemical finishes on some conventional towels.

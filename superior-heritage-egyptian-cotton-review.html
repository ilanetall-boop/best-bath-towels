Frequently Asked Questions

Is Egyptian cotton actually better than Turkish cotton for bath towels?

It depends on what you're optimizing for. Egyptian cotton has long fibers that hold more water per towel and a denser weave, while Turkish cotton has a looser weave that dries faster and tends to soften with repeated washing. Neither is objectively better, and the right choice comes down to whether you value maximum absorbency or quick drying.

How much does the SUPERIOR Heritage Egyptian Cotton set cost per towel?

SUPERIOR's two-pack bath sheet costs $57.20, or about $28.60 per towel. That's more than COTTON CRAFT's $44.99 hotel-style set and Cariloha's $44.00 bamboo viscose set, but less than BAGNO MILANO's $69.95 Turkish cotton set, so Egyptian cotton sits in the middle of this comparison on price.

Does Egyptian cotton need special care compared to regular cotton towels?

Egyptian cotton doesn't require different washing equipment, but owners report it holds up best with warm water washes and moderate dryer heat rather than high heat, which can break down the longer fibers over time. That's the same care pattern that applies to most 100% cotton towel sets, including the alternatives in this comparison.

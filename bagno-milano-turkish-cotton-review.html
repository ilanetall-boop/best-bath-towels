Frequently Asked Questions

Is Turkish cotton actually better than Egyptian cotton for bath towels?

It depends on what you're optimizing for. Turkish cotton has a looser weave that dries faster and tends to soften with repeated washing, while Egyptian cotton is denser and prized for long fibers that hold more water per towel. Neither is objectively better, and the right choice comes down to whether you value quick drying or maximum absorbency.

How much more does the Bagno Milano Turkish cotton set cost than the alternatives?

Bagno Milano's six-piece set runs $69.95, or about $11.66 per towel. SUPERIOR's Egyptian cotton set costs $57.20, COTTON CRAFT's hotel-style set costs $44.99, and Cariloha's bamboo viscose set costs $44.00, so Turkish cotton carries a real premium over the other three options here.

Does Turkish cotton need special care compared to regular cotton towels?

Turkish cotton doesn't require different washing equipment, but owners report it needs a few wash cycles before reaching its signature soft hand. Washing in warm water and avoiding excessive dryer heat helps the fibers soften evenly instead of matting, the same care pattern that applies to most 100% cotton towel sets.

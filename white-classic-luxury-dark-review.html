Is the White Classic Luxury Dark Gray bath towel worth it over a standard white towel?

How does the White Classic Luxury Dark Gray compare to the Chakir Turkish Linens sets?

Will the dark gray color on this towel fade or show lint over time?

Dark colors generally show lint more than white towels, especially during the first few washes before loose fibers shed. Washing the White Classic Luxury Dark Gray separately from lighter laundry and using a color-safe detergent cuts down on both fading and visible lint buildup over time.

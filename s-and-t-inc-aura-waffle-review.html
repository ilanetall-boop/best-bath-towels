Frequently Asked Questions

Is a waffle-weave towel actually better than terry cloth?

It depends on what you're optimizing for. Waffle weave has less surface pile, so it dries faster and packs flatter than terry cloth, while terry cloth holds more water per towel and feels plusher right out of the package. Neither is objectively better, and the right choice comes down to whether you value quick drying or maximum absorbency.

How much more does the S&T INC. Aura Waffle cost than the alternatives?

The Aura Waffle runs $38.79. One Thousand Reasons' Premium Staple Cotton towel costs $37.99, and both White Classic options cost $37.99 as well, so the waffle weave carries a small premium of about 80 cents over the three 100% cotton alternatives in this comparison.

Does a cotton blend waffle towel need different care than 100% cotton?

A cotton blend doesn't require special washing equipment, but owners report blended fibers can feel less absorbent over time than 100% cotton if washed in high heat repeatedly. Washing in warm water and avoiding excessive dryer heat helps the waffle texture keep its shape instead of flattening, the same care pattern that applies to most cotton-blend textiles.
